**Epic**: Epic 07 - Employee Dashboard Stat Cards  
**Assigned Member**: SWK Hansala (UI/UX Design & Documentation)

## 🔍 1. Requirement Gathering & Business Rules
- **Target Persona**: Employees checking their leave entitlements.
- **Core Requirements**:
  - Display summary cards for Annual, Casual, and Sick leaves.
  - Used vs Total Quota metric indicators.

---

## 🎨 2. UI/UX Design Specifications
- **Layout & Visuals**:
  - Color-coded summary cards (Green, Blue, Orange).
  - Responsive grid layout.

---

## 📝 3. User Story
*As an* employee  
*I want to* view stat cards for my leave balances on my dashboard  
*So that* I know my remaining leave entitlements  

---

## ✅ 4. Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Displays summary cards for Annual, Casual, and Sick Leave.
- [x] Displays remaining quota indicators.
